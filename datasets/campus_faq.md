# Maple Grove Learning Centre: Student FAQ

> **Fictional.** This organisation, its policies, numbers and addresses are invented for teaching. It is used as
> the "knowledge base" for the constrained question-answering assistant (Project 09, option C).

## Library

### What are the library opening hours?
Monday to Friday 08:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–16:00. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

### How many books can I borrow?
Students can borrow up to 6 books at a time for 14 days. Each book can be renewed twice online if nobody has reserved it.

### What happens if I return a book late?
The late fee is 0.50 per book per day, up to a maximum of 10.00 per book. Borrowing is paused until fees are paid.

## Exams and assessment

### When are the end-of-term exams?
End-of-term exams run in the last two weeks of each term. The exact timetable is published on the student portal four weeks before exams start.

### What should I bring to an exam?
Your student ID card, black or blue pens, and a clear water bottle. Phones must be switched off and left in your bag at the front of the room.

### I missed an exam because I was ill. What should I do?
Submit a "missed assessment" form with a medical note within 5 working days of the exam. You will usually be offered a resit in the next exam period.

### Can I submit coursework late?
Coursework submitted up to 24 hours late is capped at a pass mark. Work submitted more than 24 hours late receives zero unless an extension was approved before the deadline.

### How do I ask for an extension?
Email your course tutor before the deadline explaining the reason. Extensions are normally up to 7 days.

## Fees and money

### When are tuition fees due?
Fees are paid in three instalments: 15 September, 15 January and 15 April.

### Are there scholarships?
Yes. The Learning Centre offers merit scholarships and hardship grants. Applications open on 1 October each year and close on 31 October.

## IT and accounts

### How do I connect to the Wi-Fi?
Connect to the network "MGLC-Student" and sign in with your student email and portal password.

### I forgot my portal password.
Use the "Reset password" link on the portal login page. If you no longer have access to your recovery email, visit the IT help desk with your student ID.

### Where is the IT help desk?
Room B12 in the main building, open Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00.

## Campus life

### I lost my student ID card. What do I do?
Report it at the reception desk. A replacement card costs 5.00 and is ready the next working day.

### Is there a quiet study space?
Yes. The second floor of the library is a silent study zone, and group study rooms can be booked on the portal for up to 2 hours.

### Who do I talk to if I am struggling with my studies or wellbeing?
You can book a free, confidential appointment with the student support team at reception or on the portal.
